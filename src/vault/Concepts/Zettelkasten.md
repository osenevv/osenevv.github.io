# Zettelkasten

A note-taking method built on **atomic notes**: one idea per file, written in your own words, dated and linked to related notes. Fleeting captures get refined into permanent notes; structure emerges from links, not folders.

Why it works for engineering work: specs, trade-offs and debugging stories stay reusable. When I revisit a problem, I follow links instead of searching from scratch — same motivation as [[ZettelLinker]] and the semantic search on this site.

Practices I actually use:
- **Atomicity** — if a note needs two titles, split it.
- **Link at write time** — connect to at least one existing note; orphan notes get audited.
- **Own words** — paraphrase sources so retrieval matches how I think, not how a paper was phrased.
